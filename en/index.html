The lake

The smallest of northern Italy's great lakes, and the only one whose waters flow north. Forty minutes from Malpensa, just over an hour from Milan.

Orta San Giulio, Pella, San Maurizio d'Opaglio, Gozzano, Omegna: from one shore to the next, sunlight, access to the water, roads and prices all change. It can be the difference between a home that sells in three months and one that sits for two years — and you cannot read it from a listing.

Only a small number of sales each year, almost all between people who already know the lake.
